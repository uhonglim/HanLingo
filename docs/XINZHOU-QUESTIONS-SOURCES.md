# Xinzhou question words

This bounded collection adds **13 lexical forms** to the existing `xinzhou-jin` reference in `jin/wutai`. It introduces no locality, inferred pronunciation, recording or complete sentence transcription. Export: `xinzhouQuestionsLearning`.

## Primary evidence and locality

Ruan Jiamin 阮佳敏 (2024), “A Study on Interrogative Sentences in Xinzhou Dialect of Shanxi Province / 山西忻州方言疑问句研究”, *Modern Linguistics* 12(1):48–58, [DOI 10.12677/ml.2024.121008](https://doi.org/10.12677/ml.2024.121008). The [publisher PDF](https://pdf.hanspub.org/ml20240100000_75940526.pdf) explicitly grants **CC BY 4.0** on printed p. 49. Attribution and the link remain beside each entry.

The author reports recording conversations in January–February 2023. Exact settlements, speaker count and ages are unspecified. The paper names Xinzhou and the Wutai branch; the existing locality is already a broadly scoped named reference. Its map marker is an orientation point, not an identified recording address. No claim is made about all residents or current universal usage.

Table 1 on p. 50 supplies the phonetic forms; sections 3.1–3.9 on pp. 50–53 explain their actual grammatical meanings. These are attested question words, not character readings with inferred dictionary glosses. The fused possessive form `[suɑ31]` has no written headword in the table. Explanatory 谁家 describes its origin and is not promoted to an attested spelling: the entry stays `han: null`, `writingStatus: 'not-supplied'`.

## Numerical marks and visible qualification

The article uses bracketed phonetic transcription and IPA symbols, including [ʂ], [ɿ], [ʨ] and [ʦ]. It appends numerical groups but provides no key defining their values. Therefore:

- The ledger preserves every source segment and numerical group. Superscript numerals are flattened to baseline in `sourceForm` for a stable literal record.
- Each card retains that complete source form in its note.
- Display IPA replaces numerical groups with syllable boundaries and uses `toneNotation: 'unspecified'`. The shared converter produces segment spelling only.
- “Segments only; source number key undefined” appears beside pronunciation. No contour, named tone category or neutral-tone pitch is inferred from another Xinzhou source.
- Ligatures [ʦ] and [ʨ] remain in the source IPA. The global converter handles them; this collection defines no local spelling key.

Held table forms are 谁们 (unkeyed 0), 哪儿, 哪儿搭, 哪半儿, 哪忽栏儿 and 多会儿. The latter five involve unresolved baseline `r` notation, with uncertain nasal-vowel glyphs in two forms. No presumed trill or rhotic-vowel substitution is made.

## Meanings and practice

Usage notes preserve point-of-time versus duration, manner versus degree, restricted possessive constructions, and quantity scope. 咋地个 describes a situation or progress in the source’s exam/book examples; the gloss does not present it as a greeting.

All 13 entries remain visible words. **咋** means “how / why” and overlaps both 咋个 and 为甚. **多少** means “how much / how many” and partially overlaps 几. These two have `meaningPracticeExclude: true` so neither becomes a prompt or a supposedly wrong distractor in meaning practice. This is a quiz safeguard, not a denial of lexical evidence. 多么 and 多啦 share the same English meaning, preventing mutual distraction. The resulting collection has 11 eligible word entries and 10 distinct meaning prompts.
