# Min gallery photograph sources

Reviewed 9 October 2026. These are documentary photographs of places and daily life, not evidence of any pictured person’s language or identity.

The 14 locality galleries below contain 141 photographs in total, including 7 already-published assets. Amoy keeps its existing 11-image collection. Each gallery has 9–11 distinct images; no crops or near-identical angles were added to reach a count.

New assets are resized WebP derivatives of Wikimedia Commons photographs. Original source, creator and license are retained on every gallery item. CC BY-SA assets remain under their stated licenses independently of the application code. Raw API metadata and visual contact sheets are in the ignored `.evidence/min-galleries/` research folder.

Selection checks: locality descriptions and categories were inspected; all selected images were visually reviewed. The similarly named Zhengzhou Putian metro station and Shenzhen Xinghua Road were excluded. Unclear licenses and unavailable image derivatives were omitted. Source date fields sometimes conflict with filenames, so new gallery captions do not assert dates.
