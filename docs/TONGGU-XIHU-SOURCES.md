# Xihu, Tonggu Hakka

This collection contains **20 lexical forms** recorded from Li Sanhong’s Hakka in 2016. Ten have supplied written forms; ten remain phonetic-only. It is one consultant reference from Guancang hamlet, Xihu village, Yongning town, not a county-wide course or a representation of every village resident.

## Primary study and permission

杨加玉、曾志明、黄婷婷、杜严、项梦冰 / Jiayu Yang, Zhiming Zeng, Tingting Huang, Yan Du and Mengbing Xiang (2016), *铜鼓客家话音系 / The Phonology of Tonggu Hakka*. **Modern Linguistics 4(4):127–160**. [Official publisher PDF](https://pdf.hanspub.org/ml20160400000_58054424.pdf), DOI10.12677/ml.2016.44015. Printed127 expressly licenses the work under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The selection adapts factual lexical glosses and transcriptions; English glosses are HanLingo translations of the supplied Chinese explanations. Page images, tables and prose are not republished. The source glossary and publisher PDF remain directly linked. No recordings are claimed or supplied.

The fieldwork ran **12–27 July2016**, at Tonggu No.2 Primary School (pp128–129). Consultant Li Sanhong was41, from Guancang in Xihu. The article explicitly describes her Gan–Hakka bilingual background: after marriage she mainly used Hakka, while her home Gan had become less fluent. The authors changed their intended Gan interview to Hakka and describe contact with Gan and Mandarin. The school is the survey venue, not the consultant’s home or the map pin.

## Transcription

The paper’s phonetic font uses private-use glyphs; ordinary PDF text extraction corrupts IPA. Entries were transcribed from rendered pages139–145, joining the printed onset row, rhyme heading and tone column. The printed zero-onset row marker Ø is a table label and is omitted from the syllable transcription. Each join and Chinese gloss is preserved in `tonggu-xihu-provenance.json`.

The explicit conventional citation-tone key on printed132 is **24,113,31,44,2,5**. The authors separately discuss narrower measured contours. HanLingo preserves the published broad convention, without substituting acoustic curves or computing tone sandhi. These are supplied pitch values, not historical tone-category numbers. All selected forms use the shared IPA-to-HanLingo converter.

Source placeholders remain `han:null`. Hypothesized historical forms in commentary do not become lesson spellings. The review corrected “play” to **嫽 [liau⁴⁴]**; a separate tone24 row contains撩. No IPA or meaning changed. Homophones remain distinct lexical records: [ke³¹] “hide something” / “aphid”; [no¹¹³] “rub, knead” / “step on.” They must not become competing correct answers in a single practice question.

Held material includes whole compounds known only through constituent examples, unglossed characters without lexical definitions, a rare character whose extraction is uncertain, connected-speech examples requiring separate analysis, and apical-vowel material outside this bounded selection. The existence of a long syllabary does not establish complete vocabulary or audio coverage.
