# Hainan Min expansion

Added the Hainan Min regional branch and Wenchang locality reference. Some broad classifications subsume Hainan Min under Southern Min; the regional navigation branch is not a claim that it belongs to Tsuan-Chiang.

## Pronunciation evidence

Gongguan Peng (2026), *Wenchang Min Chinese*, Journal of the International Phonetic Association 56, e22. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/S002510032610111X. Open access, CC BY 4.0.

- 18 consonant examples from printed pp. 2–3.
- Eight tone examples plus three additional syllable-structure examples from printed p. 18.
- Original PDF pages visually inspected: its text layer corrupts both IPA and Chinese characters. The final table, not extracted text, controls transcription.
- The p. 18 tone table has **both category IDs and explicit pitch values**. The latter were matched to each example; category digits were not treated as pitch values.
- Checked tones are distinguished by final stops and explicit reading notes. Source transcriptions do not mark unreleased codas in the example table, so that mark has not been added silently.
- Two Wenchang-raised university students supplied the recordings; most acoustic measurements use the male speaker. This scope appears beside every pronunciation.
- The source discusses disagreements with earlier studies. These values are not offered as a universal municipal pronunciation.
- Implosives [ɓ ɗ], bilabial fricative [ɸ], and voiced glottal fricative [ɦ] retain their IPA distinctions. Unsupported HanLingo spellings remain unresolved.
