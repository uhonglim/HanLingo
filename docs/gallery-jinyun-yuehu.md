## Image permissions and evidence

`gallery-jinyun-yuehu-provenance.json` records every Commons source page, exact source filename, author, selected licence, licence URL, source description, capture date, original image URL, supplied camera coordinates, original SHA-256, delivered SHA-256 and transformation. Each file page explicitly offers its selected CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0 or CC BY 3.0 licence. The original licence URL is retained in provenance; the displayed licence link uses HTTPS.

The complete image is retained. EXIF orientation is applied, dimensions reduced to fit 1440 pixels and files converted to WebP. No crops or generated edits are used. The shared viewer supplies source/author/licence credit. The delivered adaptations retain their selected source licences. No photo-specific vocabulary association is invented.

All 20 original hashes, delivered hashes and source URLs are distinct. A check against existing `public/images` bytes and `src/data` source URLs found no existing duplicates. The producing agent inspected all subjects, and the root agent independently reviewed both contact sheets and geographic scope before selecting 20 final scenes. A second agent independently checked the 21-candidate set and found no visual, caption or scope mismatch; the final selection simply omits the paving-map detail. That review did not re-fetch licences. One interrupted source-image transfer was successfully retried; no HTTP 429 or access-gate bypass occurred.

## Exact scenes and selection

Jinyun leads with Dufeng Academy, followed by Heyang Gongji Bridge and the Zhu ancestral hall. The gallery also includes Lianxi stone-beam bridge, Xiandu mountain scenery, the reclining figure inside Niweng Cave, Huangdi temple, the Confucian temple's Pan pond and bridge, Heyang Caishen temple, Jinyun railway station and Huzhen's Yingbin Avenue. The road photograph appears last as modern context.

The source description specifically identifies Heyang Caishen temple, so the caption does not call it a generic residence. Niweng's selected image shows a stone figure, not just inscriptions. The Xiandu mountain photograph has no independently verified narrower peak name, so its caption keeps the source's broad location. The Pan-pond image is not labelled a main temple hall.

Yuehu leads with a dated 2012 downtown street photograph and Beiji Pavilion. Other subjects are a river view from Yingtan Bridge, the 2017 pontoon crossing, the park entrance, West Lake and the railway, the station facade, its first waiting room and a platform memorial to Xiong Yunqing. These are different scenes, not repeat views of one facade. A park paving-map detail was removed for low visual/cultural value; the gallery remains nine photographs without padding.

## Yuehu scope checks

Yingtan is also a prefecture name. This gallery does not borrow Longhushan, Guixi or Yujiang scenes. It excludes the separate Xinjiang New District overview and nonspecific prefecture photographs.

Commons supplies coordinates for the Yingtan Bridge, park, Beiji Pavilion and pontoon viewpoints. Named-site checks independently place the park, station and West Lake in Yuehu. Their primary map-provider records are linked in the provenance. A river view can include the opposite bank; captions do not claim that all visible buildings belong to Yuehu or establish language boundaries.

The downtown 2012 picture visibly includes the named People's Hospital and Linyin Road sign. Amap and the primary member-hospital directory of Futang Children’s Medical Development Research Center place the old/south hospital site at Yuehu, Shengli West Road 116. This is a documented editorial visual location match, separate from the photographer's broad “YingTan, downtown” description. No exact camera coordinate is invented. The indexed primary directory was readable; its direct request failed TLS validation, and no bypass was attempted. Amap's indexed page text supplied the addresses; direct pages returned a client shell.

Amap coordinates use a China-map coordinate system and are not directly equated to Commons WGS84. Only the Commons-supplied coordinate fields are preserved in the image ledger.

The 2012 downtown street, 2008 temple, 2016 station and 2017 river scenes remain dated historical photographs. Captions make no claim about current buildings, services, access or pontoon condition.

## Exclusions

Jinyun Shaobing photographs examined on Commons were taken outside the county—Madrid, Hangzhou, Jinhua or motorway service areas—and are not presented as Jinyun scenes. Museum objects photographed elsewhere were excluded. Yuehu's alternate bridge/pavilion angles, rail-window view, close historical portrait and park paving map were omitted. No speaker identity was inferred from appearance.
