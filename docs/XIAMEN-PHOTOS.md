# Xiamen photography

Verified on 2026-10-09. This collection adds eleven photographs for the Xiamen learning branch. The existing `public/images/xiamen-street.webp` asset is preserved and credited separately in `PHOTO-CREDITS.md`.

## Scope and editorial treatment

- Hero ID: `gulangyu-rooftops`. It shows Gulangyu in the foreground and the Xiamen waterfront across the water, viewed from Sunlight Rock. It is a 2012 photograph, not a current skyline claim.
- Places are identified from the original photographer descriptions and Wikimedia Commons records. Shuzhuang Garden is identified in the relevant Commons category. No subject’s ethnicity or spoken language is inferred from appearance.
- Years come from Commons’ original-date field where available. An absent year means no capture year is asserted. In particular, the Pexels page date is not treated as the date the photograph was taken.
- Local changes: EXIF orientation correction where present, downscaling, WebP conversion, and metadata removal. No generative edits, retouching, inserted objects, or removed people. Layouts may crop reversibly using CSS. No small source has been upscaled.
- Photo licenses are independent of the application code license. CC BY-SA photographs and their converted copies remain under their stated share-alike license.
- The Pexels photograph has the Pexels License, not a Creative Commons license. It is used within an editorial website page, not resold as stock. It must not imply endorsement or portray the subject offensively.

## Source-caption discrepancy: shop counter

The Pexels title and description refer to tea preparation. Visual inspection does not establish that activity: the person is working with small tubes or packages at a mixed-goods counter. HanLingo therefore uses a neutral shop-counter caption and the ID `shop-counter`; it should not illustrate an instruction specifically about preparing or drinking tea. The source’s title is retained below for traceability.
