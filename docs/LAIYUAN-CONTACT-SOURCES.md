# Laiyuan: two primary lexical references

The starter contains **24 lexical responses**, twelve each for Niujia and Huangzong. Twelve have source-attested Chinese writing; twelve preserve a spoken form and meaning without supplying a written form. They are lexical responses, not character readings.

## Primary language source

何純惠 / Ho Chun-Hui (2016), *客語汀州片混合方言研究—以連城縣賴源鄉方言為探討對象*. Hakka Affairs Council research report. [Official PDF](https://cloud.hakka.gov.tw/site/hakka/public/attachment/105A0010.pdf).

- Printed pp. 24, 27, 30: Tables 11-1, 12-1 and 13-1 supply the selected words.
- P. 16, note 20: digits identify historical tone categories. **67 is a merged category, not a pitch contour.** The starter preserves source digits and displays HanLingo tone labels as T1, T3, T8, T67. It does not substitute the separate phonetic tone analyses in Table 9 or infer sandhi.
- P. 29 explicitly supplies 食 for eating and drinking; pp. 31–33 support 不, 五, 睡, 水 and 耳菇/耳空. Table concept headings alone are not treated as local spellings. The proposed etymology 分 for “give” is not turned into an attested written form.
- Pp. 20 and 47–49 identify consultants and the 2016 survey. Huangzong combines consultants from Nanyang and Dakengtou natural villages. Niujia’s main consultant came from Dongkenglong natural village, with another consultant also from Niujia. The report acknowledges individual differences.

The selected Huangzong “narrow” is `[hi8]`, without an added glottal stop. Niujia has `[hieʔ8]`. The second Huangzong “ear” form is recorded in the note; the displayed entry uses the first table response.

## Classification and names

The report calls these Western Fujian mixed varieties and explicitly declines direct assignment to Min or Hakka on pp. 2 and 19. **Contact varieties → Western Fujian → Laiyuan** is HanLingo’s editorial geographic organisation, not a genealogy attributed to the source. Neither village is assigned to Hakka, Min or Tuhua by this starter.

Niujia and Huangzong are documented place labels, retained without inventing community endonyms. No source-backed local pronunciation of either place name was collected, so no secondary HanLingo name is manufactured.

The existing Liancheng county-distribution entry is broader than either village; these new references do not relabel that county sample or borrow its reading scope.

## Geography and culture

[Laiyuan’s official village record](https://www.fjlylc.gov.cn/xz/lyxrmzf/zfxxgk/zfxxgkml/30/202311/t20231123_2077220.htm) names Niujia and Huangzong separately. The report’s Fig. 2 locates the survey villages. Geographic markers use [GeoNames Niujiacun 7525880](https://www.geonames.org/7525880/niujiacun.html), 25.55008 N / 116.97338 E, and [Huangzongcun 7526393](https://www.geonames.org/7526393/huangzongcun.html), 25.52519 N / 116.93914 E. These are named-settlement anchors, not administrative boundaries, interview locations or private addresses. GeoNames gives the Longyan/Fujian hierarchy; township identity is independently matched to the report and official village record.

Huangzong’s culture topics use its [2024 historic-building protection plan](https://www.fjlylc.gov.cn/xz/lyxrmzf/zfxxgk/zfxxgkml/25/202410/t20241028_2169404.htm) and the county’s [2018 tea-garden account](https://www.fjlylc.gov.cn/ztzl/ddc/lchsy/201809/t20180913_1368622.htm). Niujia’s topics use the [2026 provincial agricultural programme](https://nynct.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/nyyw/ywgz/202602/P020260211404669667078.pdf), entry 2222, and a [2023 Fujian Daily bamboo-work report](https://fjnews.fjsen.com/wap/2023-09/14/content_31410709.htm). The latter identifies a Niujia carving workshop; other villages’ bamboo-area figures are not reassigned to Niujia.

## Reuse, transformation and review

No open licence was identified for Ho’s report. Government hosting does not establish a reuse licence. HanLingo makes a bounded factual selection with page/table attribution; it does not redistribute the PDF, scans, extended prose, source photographs or recordings. Geographic and cultural sources are separately attributed and do not establish image reuse permission.

[The provenance ledger](laiyuan-contact-provenance.json) preserves the source PDF hash, every selected form, category digits, Han locator and transformations. Legacy-font glyphs were visually transcribed; aspiration uses Unicode ʰ and nasalisation is retained. English labels follow the source glosses. Independent review checked all 24 transcription/gloss/category cells and all writing statuses against rendered source pages. Research scans and failed geographic matches remain in ignored `.evidence/laiyuan-next/`.
