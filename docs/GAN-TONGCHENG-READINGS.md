# Juanshui and Shinan character readings

This adds ten character readings each for the existing Juanshui and Shinan town references in Da–Tong Gan. It does not add a new locality, local lexical definitions, photographs or recordings. The existing source tone inventories remain separate from the twenty complete syllables.

Source: Zhang Yongsheng 张勇生 and Wang Jie 王洁, 2022, *鄂东南赣语的声调类型及其演变*, printed p.106, Table16. [Publisher volume, PDF page112](https://geolinguistics.sakura.ne.jp/Monograph/SiG-mono2-2-xia-ebook.pdf#page=112); [CC BY4.0 deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6342364). The full volume’s date is2022; a local working filename containing2021 is not the publication date.

The raw character columns and IPA are retained in `gan-tongcheng-readings-provenance.json`. Source digits are pitch values, as identified by the surrounding tone tables and discussion. The source nasal symbol ȵ remains ȵ. Source superscript digits are stored as ordinary digits and rendered by the shared pronunciation component; only brackets are added. No phonetic value is inferred from a historical category.

The earlier tone-only import excluded Table16 as a whole because it repeats 馬港 with conflicting forms. This bounded addition selects only the independently identified 隽水 and 石南 rows. **Both 馬港 rows remain excluded.** We do not guess that one was intended to say 北港. Other table rows are not silently imported.

Speaker biographies, sampling dates and exact neighbourhoods are unspecified. Town anchors remain geographic orientation. The source gives comparative character readings rather than contextual lexical definitions, so every record is `character-reading`, excluded from meaning quizzes. Spelling practice deduplicates identical readings such as 塔 and 达. General written-character senses, where available, remain explicitly separate from local meanings.

The two towns preserve internal differences: 答 is [tanʔ55] in Juanshui and [taiʔ55] in Shinan; 节 has [tɕiɛʔ55] versus [tɕiɛnʔ55]. These do not establish a uniform county pronunciation or a rule for all words.

## Culture sources

Juanshui’s opera garden is documented in a [6 January2025 Xianning Daily report republished by the Hubei housing department](https://zjt.hubei.gov.cn/bmdt/dtyw/szsm/202501/t20250106_5491125.shtml). Its location is explicitly Xianghan community, Yanhe Road. The [county cultural-service directory](https://tcxwly.chaoxing.com/) names the museum at Yinshan Culture and Art Centre in Juanshui; its indexed source text supplied the address when direct retrieval timed out.

Shinan’s gong-and-light Lantern Festival is described by the [provincial intangible-heritage centre](https://wlt.hubei.gov.cn/hbsfwzwhycw/mtgz/xwdt/202102/t20210226_3364961.shtml), with Meigang village as applicant community. A [18 June2024 Xianning Daily firsthand report](https://app.xnnews.com.cn/news/rt/202406/t20240618_3624040.shtml) traces clapping dance to Shinan and Daping and describes its later teaching; indexed publisher text was available when direct web-tool access failed. These cultural accounts do not establish the linguistic consultants’ identities or homes. No photographs are copied from these reports.

Independent visual review confirmed all twenty character/segment/pitch joins against PDF page112. The exact-pair review is `.evidence/whole-atlas-next/gan-table16-independent-depth-review.json`. No conflicting Magang form was released.
