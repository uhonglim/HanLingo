# Whole-atlas expansion

The 2026-10-09 request expands HanLingo beyond its original five groups. Gan, Xiang, Jin, Hui and Pinghua are first-class entries. Tuhua is a separately labelled regional collection; the interface does not assert that its member communities form one genealogical group.

## Completion standard

A locality is developed only when its own evidence supports a useful set of words, exact source IPA, the shared HanLingo spelling, sound explanations, cultural material and an attributed gallery. Amoy and the wider Tsuân-Tsiang collection remain the depth references. An administrative distribution row, a link to a neighbour, or a generated translation is not a lesson.

Use `npm run audit:content` to count all localities, including those with zero learning coverage. The count-based Amoy benchmark is a minimum inventory check, not a quality score. Check meaningful everyday vocabulary and regional distinctions separately. Keep photographic placements separate from distinct assets and live speaker recordings separate from isolated IPA demonstrations.

## Work sequence

1. Remove fixed-five assumptions from the navigation, homepage, maps, group articles, spelling workshop and content audit. Retain the existing six comparison targets until additional translation targets have their own pipeline evidence.
2. Introduce sourced branches and locality pages for the additional groups. Preserve source ranks and label geographic collections. Do not replace the original 1,000 references or their routes.
3. Establish substantive pronunciation collections and documentary galleries for core new localities. Source date and survey community stay visible beside each reading. Historical surveys cannot be described as new recordings or universal current usage.
4. Extend each new branch and the existing branches with locality-specific evidence. Add a name reading only when sourced IPA supports it; a missing local reading is preferable to a pinyin-derived invention.
5. Add recorded words and phrases where the original recording and reuse conditions are available. The IPA sound tool demonstrates supported individual sounds and does not claim to synthesize an unrecorded dialect.
6. Verify every published batch: source/content diff, IPA conversion, routes, map/tree interactions, photo credits, desktop/mobile use, tests and build. Record deployed source revision separately from local work.

## Source handling

- Prefer primary surveys, dictionaries and recording collections. A licensed scholarly transcription dataset is an edition of its cited source, not a fresh field survey.
- Pin imported dataset versions, preserve original forms and document every transformation. Do not silently convert tone-category digits to pitch contours.
- Respect unavailable or blocked source hosts; use independent accessible sources or preserve the gap.
- Cultural photographs show their named scene. They do not establish a photographed person's language, and a city scene must not be captioned as a different village.
