# Gongjiang photo gallery

Nine dated scenes develop the Gongjiang Town Hakka reference in Yudu County. They show named architecture, streets, railway infrastructure and riverfront places. None identifies a linguistic consultant or proves the language spoken by a pictured person.

## Selection and locality

- Historic church building, He family house, Xie ancestral hall, Guan family house and Zhaozhong Hall: five distinct old-town structures.
- Hongqi Avenue: ordinary traffic and shopfronts, January 2017.
- Yudu station platform: railway infrastructure, February 2018. Later station reconstruction is not depicted or inferred.
- Memorial museum and garden path, and East Gate river landing: distinct building and riverbank subjects within the larger memorial garden, May 2014. The photographed museum structure is not presented as a newly built museum or as evidence of current operation.

Seven photos date to May 2014. Every image keeps its photographer, licence, exact Commons file page and date. The selection excludes alternate angles, duplicate monuments, repeated plaques and unidentified panoramas. A government aerial was excluded because its uploader-applied Commons licence did not establish the original rights holder’s permission.

The six Commons camera anchors locate photographed scenes, not consultants. Three files have no camera coordinates: Hongqi Avenue, Yudu station and Xie hall. Their exact named subjects are joined to primary locality evidence; no camera position is invented.
