# Min branch learning evidence

Verified 9 October 2026. These packs extend Eastern Min, Northern Min, Puxian, and Central Min. They do not replace the detailed Amoy lesson pack.

## Pronunciation

- **Fuzhou: 8 character readings.** [CUHK Fuzhou syllable index](https://humanum.arts.cuhk.edu.hk/Lexis/lexi-mf/dialectIndex.php?point=O), rows 210, 632, 726, 912, 1070, 1216, 1424. The university database supplies IPA segments and pitch contours separately. We join those fields, render pitch digits as IPA Chao tone letters, and expand the legacy affricate ligature ʦ to t͡s. We do not infer phrases or sandhi. 茶 ta53, 水 tsuoi32, 魚 ŋy53, 米 mi32, 手 tshieu32, 山 saŋ44, 三 saŋ44, 日 niʔ5.
- **Jian’ou: 8 character readings.** [CUHK Jian’ou syllable index](https://humanum.arts.cuhk.edu.hk/Lexis/lexi-mf/dialectIndex.php?point=Q), rows 287, 376, 620, 932, 1301, 1351, 1358, 1366. 茶 ta33, 水 sy21, 魚 ŋy33, 米 mi21, 人 neiŋ33, 手 siu21, 山 suiŋ54, 三 saŋ54. These are selected character readings, not an assertion that every character has only one reading. The database includes alternate readings for some other characters; those were not flattened into one invented norm.
- **Putian: 10 attested forms.** [Wu 2021, Puxian subgrouping](https://www.ling.sinica.edu.tw/upload/researcher_manager_result/17e7b4c054664b1e85670eb38147af5b.pdf), printed pp. 160–163, tables 1–5. Readings transcribed from the Putian row only. Table 4 explicitly maps category 1→533, 2→13, 3→453, 5→42, 6→11. Those mappings are disclosed in the UI. ts and tsh are normalized to t͡s and t͡sʰ; plain h used as aspiration in the source becomes superscript ʰ. PDF pages 6–8 were rendered and visually checked to avoid text-extraction errors. The source's [ɬ] must not become [l] or [s].
- **Yong’an: no word list yet.** [Wu and Lin 2007](https://www.ling.sinica.edu.tw/upload/researcher_manager_result/80f9788d396d35b0e8c32ebd19416ac5.pdf), pp. 1–2, visually checked. This is comparative historical evidence of endings, not a contemporary phrasebook. We retain sound notes and study material rather than assign tones to isolated reconstructed or historical forms.

CUHK's separate character definitions supply the ordinary English glosses; the cards are explicitly labelled dictionary character readings. No generated speech or locally hosted recording is claimed. CUHK links include their own speaker controls: direct Fuzhou audio endpoint `dialect/O/O00002.mp3` was verified as HTTP 200 audio/mpeg. Their recordings remain on the university site; we do not redistribute them. We did not substitute Singapore Fuzhou for Fuzhou city or Fuqing for Fuzhou.

## Further study

[Peng, A Phonetic Study of Fuzhou Chinese](https://lbms03.cityu.edu.hk/theses/c_ftt/phd-ctl-b4086098xf.pdf) was retrieved and its table 2.1 on printed p. 11 visually inspected. Its experiments distinguish citation forms, speaker measurements, and connected tones. We link the study but do not mix its experimental contours with the CUHK word set.

[Wu 2023](https://www.ling.sinica.edu.tw/upload/researcher_manager_result/c37535b570fa1f44797850580f9ef3ba.pdf), pp. 73–77, supplies Northern Min rhyme correspondences. Its numbered categories were not mistaken for pitch contours. The older [Lien abstract](https://thjcs.site.nthu.edu.tw/p/406-1452-41564%2Cr2998.php?Lang=zh-tw) describes competing Jian’ou rhyme systems, but the accessible page was not a full-text word source.

## Culture and photographs

- Fuzhou's [municipal lacquer account](https://www.fuzhou.gov.cn/zgfzzt/zjrc/mdfc/msfq/201308/t20130809_129757.htm) documents cloth-and-lacquer construction and mould removal. The [municipal historic-district publication](https://swj.fuzhou.gov.cn/zwgk/swgz/202409/P020240906466809991566.pdf) identifies Three Lanes and Seven Alleys.
- [Jian’ou banner balancing](https://www.ihchina.cn/Article/Index/detail?id=13779) is documented by the national intangible-heritage registry. Only equipment, movement, and musical-accompaniment facts are paraphrased; no unsupported origin narrative is reproduced.
- [Mazu belief and customs](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/mazu-belief-and-customs-00227) is UNESCO's primary entry and includes a film. The location is explicitly Meizhou Island within Putian, not a claim that the pictured ritual or speech represents the urban speech of every Putian resident.
- [Yong’an Daqiang opera](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/13157/) is located in Fengtian, Qingshui She township. We explicitly distinguish its performance tradition from urban Yong’an conversation.
