# Mainland Min gallery expansion

Reviewed 9 October 2026. Images are documentary place references, not evidence of the speech or identity of pictured people. Some galleries include towns and villages within the named municipality or county; those locations are explicit in captions. Shaxian bianrou is a documented dish illustration, not an asserted local restaurant scene.

Source Commons description pages were read for location, author and license. Files were resized to at most 1440 pixels and converted to WebP; no crops, retouching or generated imagery. Individual derivative images remain under their stated source license. Original download SHA-256 hashes are recorded below. Contact sheets were reviewed for scene diversity and correspondence with captions.
