# Wu and Hakka locality expansion — 2026-10-09

Adds seven locality references: Hangzhou, Ningbo, Shaoxing, Linhai, Wuhua, Xingning and Dabu. Adds the Taizhou branch under Wu. Export contract: `src/data/expansion/wu-hakka.ts` → `wuHakkaExpansion`.

Every locality has two article sections, two sourced sound/learning notes, two culture topics and at least two external references. These are locality introductions and research paths, not new IPA lessons. No IPA, pitch contour or trial romanization was inferred from a geographical name, neighboring variety or source spelling.

## Research actually read

- Hangzhou: Xu Yue, **Dialects in Hangzhou**, municipal English site (2018), full article. Old-city Wu, neighboring Yuhang and Xiaoshan, and southwest village scope are distinguished. UNESCO West Lake entry read for landscape context.
- Ningbo: Fang Hu, **An acoustic and articulatory analysis of vowels in Ningbo Chinese**, ICPhS 2003 PDF. Ten male city speakers in the 2001 acoustic sample; two articulatory participants. Vowel lip configurations and short-vowel/glottal-closure contrasts are described without manufacturing word readings.
- Shaoxing: Wen, Chen & Cheng, **Disyllabic tone sandhi patterns in Shaoxing Wu Chinese**, ICPhS 2023. Both syllable positions, register and contour matter. Local government culture pages support boat and food topics.
- Linhai/Taizhou: Phil Rose, **Dialect-geographical acoustic-tonetics**, Interspeech 2018 PDF, especially subgroup map and Genglou/Linhai discussion. Genglou speaker findings remain labeled Genglou; no transfer to every urban resident. Linhai local gazetteer page identifies the historic city’s four areas; Yongquan citrus is explicitly wider-municipality context.
- Wuhua: Hsu Fan-ping’s author summary in NCU Hakka College E-paper 139 (2011), read directly. Distinguishes north/south phonology from east/west vocabulary; Taiwan origin communities are Anliu and Meilin. Wuhua county theatre account read for puppet and tea-picking drama.
- Xingning: **A comparative study of Taiwan Miaoli and Guangdong Xingning Hakka**, official Hakka Affairs Council hosted thesis, indexed abstract and contents read. North/south differences, consonant codas and geographically varying suffixes stay separate from Miaoli. Xingning cultural bureau and local gazetteer pages support houses and lantern gatherings.
- Dabu: county government **Dabu Hakka dialect** (2024), indexed full article read. Huliao/Baihou and four other local areas are distinguished. Written visiting expressions are research leads, not converted to IPA. County culture account supports architectural variety and family food practices.

## Deliberate gaps

- Shanghang is not published: available primary phonological research explicitly concerns Gutian town, while the urban center’s placement differs between classification schemes. Gutian evidence is insufficient to assign the urban point.
- Jingxian is not published: the local government account identifies Mandarin influence/communities in the county seat, so county-wide Xuanzhou Wu evidence cannot simply label the urban marker.
- Jinhua/Wuzhou is not published: Wuzhou is independently present in Rose’s classification, but nine documented distinct urban Jinhua photos were not assembled.
- Ningdu and Tonggu are not published: no adequate distinct photo set was found in this pass. A primary 2016 Tonggu phonology study was read and specifies a bilingual woman from Guancang, Xihu village, Yongning—not a uniform county accent. This is a useful future evidence lead, not a new current lesson.
- New word/IPA lessons remain a separate source-transcription task. More scenery does not close that vocabulary gap.
