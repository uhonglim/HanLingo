## Subjects and exact scope

**Changzhi:** Shangdang Gate; Lu’an City God Temple; Qiaobei snack street; the rebuilt Liufu Tower; Taihang Park’s north entrance; city library; new city museum; a duck-shaped bronze censer displayed at that museum; a route13 bus explicitly photographed in Luzhou District; the sports arena; a geolocated residential street. These are separate subjects, not eleven angles of a landmark. The museum object is labelled as a display and kept distinct from its excavation provenance. Liufu Tower is described as rebuilt because the source explicitly calls it new. These city scenes do not establish the identity or speech of photographed people.

**Handan:** Xuebu Bridge; Congtai Park; mosque; Grand Theatre; library interior; museum; Fuyang Park’s rock garden; paths/planting at Longtai in the Zhao Kingdom Site Park; Handandao historic district; a bowl of wontons; Handan Post entrance. The gallery represents the named city’s cultural setting. It does **not** establish a connection to the **Mazhuang** speaker reference in the phonetic study, nor does the Congtai map anchor represent that recording location.

The wonton source file is explicitly titled *Handan wonton* and categorised by its contributor as food in Handan. Its short description only says “wonton”. The caption preserves this limited attestation: no restaurant, neighbourhood, uniquely local recipe or speaker identity is claimed. A separate Nanyancun-noodle candidate is excluded because its source describes only the wider Handan region.

**Tunxi additions:** the Binyuan street gate at the southwest corner of Cheng Dawei’s former residence, and Zhenhai Bridge in April2013. The gate is named precisely rather than presented as a full residence interior. The bridge image is dated; no claim is made about its current fabric. These subjects differ from the existing Liyang residence and modern pedestrian suspension bridge.

## Attribution, rights and image processing

[gallery-jin-tunxi-provenance.json](gallery-jin-tunxi-provenance.json) preserves each source filename, description, author, Commons page, direct original URL, source date, available camera coordinates, selected licence/link, original SHA256 and delivered asset SHA256. The file pages explicitly license the selected images under **CC BY-SA4.0**, **CC BY-SA3.0**, **CC BY3.0** or **CC0**. Author credits and HTTPS licence links remain part of every displayed image record. Where Commons supplied an HTTP Creative Commons link, its identical HTTPS endpoint is used for display; the original link is retained as `sourceLicenseUrl` in provenance.

All24 source pages, original-file hashes and delivered WebP hashes are distinct. None of the new WebP assets duplicates an existing published image. Processing applied EXIF orientation, resized to fit1440pixels, and converted to WebP. No crop, generative edit, scene replacement or inferred cultural activity was introduced.

The original images, Commons page cache, selection history, held candidates and three reviewed contact sheets remain in ignored `.evidence/gallery-next/`. Downloads were bounded to metadata-checked candidates, used two workers, and encountered no rate limits. The held second Changzhi museum object was replaced with an everyday bus scene for subject variety; it was not rejected as a falsely located display.
