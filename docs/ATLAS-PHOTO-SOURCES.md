# Atlas photograph expansion — Wenling, Yueqing and Hefei

Added 27 photographs: nine per locality, with six documented culture topics. The Wenling and Yueqing additions are culture collections, not completed IPA courses. Hefei’s pronunciation material is maintained separately.

## Scope and selection

The speech anchors remain urban Wenling and Lecheng in Yueqing. These galleries also show explicitly named nearby towns within their municipalities. Shitang, Xinhe, Baishi, Nanyue and northern Yueqing are not silently treated as the same speech community as the anchor. Captions do not infer the language, ethnicity or identity of pictured people.

Commons category lists were obtained through the MediaWiki `categorymembers` API; every selected file was checked with `imageinfo` (`url`, `size`, `extmetadata`). Description, author, licence and licence URL were retained. Sources were downloaded and resized proportionally to a maximum 1,600 pixels, then encoded as WebP; no crops or generative changes were made. CC BY / CC BY-SA attribution and licence links accompany every image in the gallery.

Contact sheets and the replacement tidal-barrage image were visually inspected. Rejected candidates: the close-up Wenhua Bridge pillar, a dark memorial-tablet detail, a hazy wind-farm view, and an additional view of the same Yueqing old-town waterway. They are not included or counted. The retained Changyu image is correctly captioned as the quarry-site entrance, not a cavern interior.

## Culture sources

- Taizhou Local Gazetteer, [温岭景区](https://tzsz.zjtz.gov.cn/art/2018/12/13/art_1229209654_54440083.html), sections 长屿硐天 and 石塘: former quarrying, stone architecture and the named towns. Also used to identify Donghui Pavilion as a modern park structure rather than claiming an ancient original.
- Wenling municipal government, [Changyu Dongtian project description](https://www.wl.gov.cn/art/2013/1/10/art_1544447_27478587.html): geographic setting and the quarry site.
- China Intangible Cultural Heritage Digital Museum, [Yueqing fine-line paper-cutting](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/13929/): regional craft distribution, dragon-lantern origins, tools and motifs.
- China Intangible Cultural Heritage Digital Museum, [Yueqing boxwood carving](https://www.ihchina.cn/project_details/14024): figure carving, root carving and splitting techniques. Search-indexed primary text was available; the direct page timed out during one access attempt.
- Ministry of Culture, [Yueqing heritage exhibition](https://www.mct.gov.cn/whzx/tpxw/200606/t20060621_828325.htm), 2006: independent primary confirmation of both crafts and their local institutions.

## Pronunciation evidence boundary

Targeted Cambridge/JIPA searches did not establish a Wenling or Yueqing Illustration suitable for transcribing new lessons in this pass. No words or IPA were invented. A search returned Shi & Chen’s [Lili Wu Chinese](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0025100320000092), but Lili is a separate town in Wujiang, not Jiaxing, Wenling or Yueqing; its readings were not transferred.

Integration exports: `atlasExtraGalleries` from `src/data/galleries/atlas-extra.ts`; `atlasCulturePacks` from `src/data/learning/atlas-culture.ts`. Merge the culture/resource arrays with the existing branch packs, preserving locality IDs and existing phonetic material.

## Hefei addition

Nine distinct subjects were retained after visual inspection. The Nanfei River photograph was rejected because a train window and bridge obstruct the scene; the dim Dashushan view and a dry-stream bridge view in Yaohai Park were also omitted. The Li residence exterior replaces an initially downloaded exhibition-relief detail. These rejected images are not shipped or counted. No photograph is used to infer a resident’s language.

Primary culture references:

- [Bao Gong shrine: municipal cultural account](https://m.ccdi.gov.cn/content/68/00/13456.html): the shrine on Xianghuadun, halls, inscriptions and surrounding water; article attributed to Hefei municipal publicity staff and Baohe’s Bao Gong culture association. - [Anhui Museum](https://www.ahm.cn/): official distinction between the Luyang building on Anqing Road and the Shushan building on Huaining Road.
- [Anhui Museum: Huizhou architectural collection](https://www.ahm.cn/News/Details/fwzl?nid=14782): the museum’s account of wood, brick and stone architectural objects from Huizhou. The culture text explicitly distinguishes a collection held in Hefei from objects originating in Hefei.
