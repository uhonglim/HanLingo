# Xinzhou photo gallery

Nine photographs develop the existing `xinzhou-jin` city reference. They document urban Xinzhou in Xinfu District, not the unspecified consultant settlements in Ruan’s 2023 linguistic fieldwork. No photographed speaker identity, uniform city speech, construction age, original architectural fabric, or present operation is inferred.

## Selection

The gallery includes Gongchen Gate, the courtyard and theatre stage of Xiurong Academy, three separately named temples, the old normal-university campus, Xinzhou railway station and a bus at Tushaomen. The academy photographs show different subjects and functions rather than alternate views of one building. A second station photograph and a hotel facade were held; nine distinct scenes are preferable to filling an eleven-photo target.

All photographs date to June–July 2025. Every selected image has source camera coordinates within the urban city reference. Dates and attribution come from the exact Commons file pages. No local-word association has been added: the current question-word pack does not name these photographed subjects.

## Review and checks

The producing agent and parent visually reviewed the selected images. An independent peer checked source metadata, captions and locality scope before implementation. The nine source URLs, original-image hashes and delivered-image hashes are distinct, with no delivered-byte matches against existing photo assets at preparation time.

The focused gallery test verifies asset provenance, licences, date and camera scope, omission of held photographs, and absence of unsupported reading associations. The shared gallery test and TypeScript check are run before handoff. These photographs improve cultural coverage; they do not supply additional verified pronunciation.
