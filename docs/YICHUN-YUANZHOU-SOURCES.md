# Yichun · central–southern Yuanzhou: sixteen lexical forms

## Primary source and scope

Xuping Li. 2018. *A Grammar of Gan Chinese: The Yichun Language*. Sinitic Languages of China 1. De Gruyter Mouton. [DOI 10.1515/9781501507328](https://doi.org/10.1515/9781501507328). [Official publisher preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781501507328_A33481764/preview-9781501507328_A33481764.pdf).

Printed p. 6 places the Yuanzhou variety in **Yi–Liu Gan**. Pages 9–10 narrow the investigated variety to **central–southern Yuanzhou**, one of four broad local phonetic divisions. It is not the speech of every resident of Yuanzhou District, and it is not a prefecture-wide Yichun reference. The existing locality `gan-county-360902` is retained as the district reference; the reading register explicitly states the narrower source scope. No new point, duplicate locality, consultant address or linguistic boundary is inferred.

The acknowledgements identify Rao Xing, a native local retired university teacher, as the principal consultant. The book project ran during 2009–2013; additional checking by native speaker Liu Xing began in September 2016. These are documentary dates, not an invented single recording year. **2018 is publication year.** Some examples derive from local television material, so the starter calls them published lexical examples rather than a uniform recording session.

## Selection, transcription and review

The sixteen records have actual source meanings, written forms and pronunciations. They are **words**, not unglossed homophone-table characters. Exact references are recorded in [yichun-yuanzhou-provenance.json](yichun-yuanzhou-provenance.json): printed pp. 30, 31, 39, 40 and 43. The full PDF’s physical page number is printed page plus 19.

- 老兄 and 老弟 have the kinship meanings supplied by the author; 老 is respectful/familiar here.
- 表哥、表弟、表妹 retain the source’s restricted cousin category, through maternal relatives or the father’s sisters.
- 树、车、刀 are separately printed free nouns. Their forms are not guessed by stripping an obligatory suffix.
- 迷毛雨 is permitted because both the source Han and IPA explicitly make the diminutive suffix optional.
- 苹果 and 菜 retain pronunciations in the published sentence/phrase examples. No measured isolated citation form is claimed.
- 我、你、渠 preserve the exact source pronoun forms, including nasal **ȵ** and written **渠**. No unsupported gender is attached to third person.

Printed p. 18 explicitly defines Chao five-level pitch contours **34, 44, 42, 213 and 5**. These are not tone-category labels. P. 19 states that entering-tone 5 is omitted in stop-final examples. None of the sixteen selected forms requires restoring such a tone. Affricate aspiration is encoded with modifier ʰ; superscript pitch digits and within-compound spacing are typographic normalization. Source vowels **y/œ**, nasal **ȵ** and every supplied contour are preserved. The shared converter alone generates HanLingo spelling.

Held out: 表姐’s explicit sandhi chain, 洋火 with a tone chain and an unprinted final tone, plural pronouns with untoned suffix li, and the suffixed 树仔 form. A separate 2019 diminutive paper uses other contour conventions; its values are not mixed into this book’s records. No audio, reconstructed sandhi or community review is claimed.

A second reader independently checked all sixteen Han/IPA/gloss pairs, the optional suffix, free nouns, pronouns, exact locality scope and tone convention against rendered primary pages. No transcription hold remains in this selection.
