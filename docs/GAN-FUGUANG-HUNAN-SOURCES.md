# Hunan township Fu–Guang starter

Sixteen comparative character readings from **抚州湖南**, Chen Nuo 2026, printed p.110 Table 2. This is Hunan Township in the Linchuan area of Fuzhou, Jiangxi, not Hunan province or Fujian Fuzhou. The records are character readings; no everyday lexical definitions, phrase lessons, local recordings or place-name pronunciation are inferred.

## Tone marks

The source prints Chinese corner tone-category marks. The [Unicode A700 chart](https://www.unicode.org/charts/PDF/UA700.pdf) identifies them by category. HanLingo retains each original form in its note and normalizes the category marker only for its existing source-category renderer:

| Source mark | Code point | Category | Editorial identifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ꜀ | U+A700 | 陰平 | T1 |
| ꜁ | U+A701 | 陽平 | T2 |
| ꜂ | U+A702 | 陰上 | T3 |
| ꜄ | U+A704 | 陰去 | T5 |
| ꜆ | U+A706 | 陰入 | T7 |

These T identifiers are **editorial category identifiers**: neither printed source digits, local numbering nor pitch contours. Exact segment strings are unchanged. For example, source `꜀tsai` is stored as `[tsai1]` with `toneNotation: source-category`, yielding `tsai·T1`; its original form and category explanation remain beside the reading. There is no complete local tone inventory or pitch assignment here. The source’s [t] versus [ʔ] final distinction remains visible.

Practice uses spelling recognition only, with equal-spelling 栽/斋 and 三/山 treated as duplicates for distractor purposes. Character meanings are not invented.
