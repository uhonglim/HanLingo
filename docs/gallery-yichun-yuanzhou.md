# Yuanzhou urban photography

Ten distinct scenes are published for the existing `gan-county-360902` reference: Qiaolou, the Confucian temple’s Dacheng Hall, Nanchan Pavilion, Zhuangyuan Pavilion and Islet, Changli Pavilion, Yuanzhou Bridge, a lake footbridge, a city shopping corner, Yichun railway station and the broadcasting centre.

The photographs illustrate **urban Yuanzhou/Yichun**, not the other counties of Yichun prefecture. They do not identify consultants or authenticate the central–southern Yuanzhou pronunciations. No photo-related word association has been invented.

Per-image source pages, exact original filenames/descriptions, author credits, selected Creative Commons licences, capture or upload dates, available camera coordinates and source/asset hashes are in [gallery-yichun-yuanzhou-provenance.json](gallery-yichun-yuanzhou-provenance.json). Source pages expressly state CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0 or CC BY 3.0. The chosen licence is shown beside each photograph and has an HTTPS licence link.

The set has **10 unique original hashes and 10 unique WebP hashes**. All scenes were reviewed on a contact sheet and checked against file metadata. It contains separate buildings and everyday urban settings, not crops or repeated angles of one landmark. The lake footbridge’s park name and the shopping corner’s street name are not supplied by the photographer; the captions preserve those gaps. Several Panoramio records provide only upload dates; no capture year is invented for them.

A tempting vegetable-garden image in the same broad Commons category was excluded: its camera coordinate is far west of the urban set, so it does not support the intended city context. Other Yichun prefecture counties and generic regional food photographs were not substituted.

Processing applied EXIF orientation, resized to fit 1440 pixels and converted to WebP. No crop, generative edit or scene replacement was made. The scene/contact-sheet/source research is retained under ignored `.evidence/gan-yiliu-next/photos/`; licensed public assets use `/images/yichun-yuanzhou-*.webp`.
